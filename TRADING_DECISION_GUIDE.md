# Trading Decision Guide — MarketPulse V3

Visual guide explaining MarketPulse decision states and trading discipline.

---

## 🎨 Decision State Badges

### 🟢 BUY NOW
- **Meaning**: All critical validation gates passed. High-conviction entry confirmed.
- **Criteria**: Master Score $\ge 80$, Judge = BUY, Confidence $\ge 7/10$, R:R $\ge 1.5$, Data Quality $\ge 75\%$, Market regime permits, Not extended ($<10\%$).

### 🟡 BUY ON CONFIRMATION
- **Meaning**: Strong setup identified, but waiting for price trigger confirmation.
- **Criteria**: Master Score $\ge 75$, trigger price specified (e.g., BUY ONLY ABOVE ₹2,850.00).

### 🔵 WATCH
- **Meaning**: Interesting setup, but lacks volume or technical confirmation.
- **Criteria**: Master Score $65\text{--}74$.

### 🔴 AVOID
- **Meaning**: Weak technical structure, low RVOL, bad risk/reward, or overextended move.
- **Action**: WAIT / DO NOT BUY.

### 🟠 BLOCKED
- **Meaning**: Setup is technically attractive, but blocked by a risk gate (e.g. Portfolio Concentration limit).
- **Action**: DO NOT TRADE.

### ⚪ NO TRADE
- **Meaning**: Default state when no NSE stock in the universe passes quality filters.
- **Action**: NO HIGH-CONVICTION BUY TODAY.
